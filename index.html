About Me

I'm Dhanush Mohanasundaram, a final-year B.E. Electronics and Communication Engineering student at Velalar College of Engineering and Technology, pursuing a minor in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science.

My career direction combines software engineering, backend systems, and applied artificial intelligence. I am building a strong foundation in Java, data structures and algorithms, object-oriented programming, databases, API development, and computer science fundamentals while exploring how AI can make software more intelligent and useful.

My technical interests extend across full-stack development, computer vision, embedded-to-cloud integration, AI-powered applications, and system architecture. I enjoy breaking down complex problems, designing practical solutions, and learning how individual components work together in complete systems.

Beyond development, I have participated in Smart India Hackathon initiatives, completed an AI internship at Edu-versity, and contributed to technical community activities through IEEE Communications Society at my college.

I believe strong engineers do more than write code. They understand the problem, design the solution, evaluate trade-offs, test their implementation, and communicate their decisions clearly.
